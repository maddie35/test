Sample 04 · information architecture · 2025

Complete overhaul of the Affirm developer documentation site

The site had grown by accretion: every product added its own section, and nothing was ever removed. Merchants could not find the page they needed, and internal teams could not tell where a new page belonged. I rebuilt the structure around what a merchant is trying to do.

Audience

Merchant developers and internal teams

Method

Content audit, card sort, templatized page types, user interviews

Year

2025
